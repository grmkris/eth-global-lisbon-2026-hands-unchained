01 · The platform

Plain HTTP, end to end

50 ms control, 8 fps MJPEG, curl-debuggable — no WebRTC, no SDKs. Rigs only dial out, so a venue firewall is not a demo risk.

Safety is structural: a 15°/tick clamp, a 0.5 s deadman, owner-calibrated servo limits, and an e-stop anyone watching can hit.
