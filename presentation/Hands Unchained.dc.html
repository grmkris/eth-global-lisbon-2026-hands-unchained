02 · Paying strangers

The gate is a person

Not reputation theater — this is paid physical labor. Every World ID holder is 18 or over by World's own policy, so the age floor rides along with proof of personhood. And the nullifier binds one human to one payout account: fifty wallets cannot farm the same market.

02 · Paying strangers

Claimed is not graded

Claimed

The operator clicks success. Today that is the only vote on the work — and it belongs to the person being paid for it.

Graded

0G Compute reads the trajectory: duration, joint travel, gripper cycles, whether an episode actually saved. Disagreement means no payment.

That hole is real and pre-existing, which is what makes a referee load-bearing instead of decorative. So we demo it on purpose: botch an attempt, claim success, watch the payout not happen.

02 · Paying strangers

The demo, in seven beats

We open on the deny path — an unverified browser is refused the lease. Then I verify on my own phone, drive one attempt on the arm in front of you, and let the referee decide whether it pays.
